# Environment Summary
Last updated: 2026-07-10 16:12:24

1. **Gravity-Driven Vertical Descent**: Constant downward acceleration requires the main engine (2) to modulate `y_vel` to manage descent velocity and prevent high-energy impacts.
2. **Main Engine Torque Induction**: Firing the main engine (2) when the craft's `angle` is non-zero generates immediate and significant angular acceleration (`ang_vel`).
3. **Side-Engine Lateral-Angular Coupling**: Side engines (1 and 3) provide lateral thrust to manage `x_vel` but induce significant angular torque, which can destabilize the craft's orientation if not applied with precision.
4. **Angular Momentum Feedback Loop**: High `ang_vel` drives rapid changes in `angle`, which in turn increases the torque magnitude generated by the main engine during descent, creating a self-reinforcing cycle of instability.
5. **The "Main Engine Trap"**: Using the main engine (2) to counteract vertical descent while the craft is tilted often exacerbates angular deviation, transforming manageable tilts into terminal rotation.
6. **Angular Velocity Divergence**: Once `ang_vel` reaches high magnitudes, the torque provided by side engines (1 and 3) becomes insufficient to effectively stabilize the `angle`, leading to rapid orientation divergence.
7. **Lateral Velocity Persistence**: High `x_vel` creates significant horizontal drift that is difficult to neutralize during the final descent phase, especially when orientation is unstable.
8. **Contact-Induced Angular Explosion**: The transition to leg contact (`left_leg_contact` or `right_leg_contact` = 1.0) triggers an immediate, non-linear escalation of `ang_vel`, where existing rotation is rapidly amplified by the mechanical interaction with the ground.
9. **Ground-Contact Fulcrum Effect**: Once in contact, any engine thrust (1, 2, or 3) generates extreme torque by leveraging against the contact point, far exceeding the torque magnitude generated during free-flight.
10. **Contact-State Equilibrium Fragility**: Once in contact, the system enters a high-gain regime where maintaining a stable orientation is extremely difficult; even minor corrective actions or "nothing" actions (0) can trigger large-magnitude angular fluctuations and significant reward penalties due to the extreme torque-to-thrust ratio.
